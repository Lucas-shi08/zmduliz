01 — What we do

R&D to production,
for other people's brands.

ZEMING develops and manufactures car audio platforms for the global aftermarket — component and coaxial speakers, tweeters, mid and full-range units, subwoofers, underseat subwoofers, DSP amplifiers and sound damping materials.

The business is OEM and ODM: brands, importers and distributors bring the market and the positioning, and we cover the engineering, production and the documentation behind the product. Final specifications, fitment and packaging are confirmed against the selected model before quotation.

We publish what the model files support. Where a parameter is not confirmed for a platform, it is left out rather than estimated — the approved sample governs the order.

02 — What OEM / ODM means here

You own the brand.
We own the build.

A programme starts with a market, a buyer and a price position — not with a sketch. From there we align the platform, review acoustic and structural requirements, and validate samples against the confirmed specification.

Branding, appearance, acoustic tuning and packaging are reviewed per project, and feasibility is confirmed before sampling. Production then follows the approved configuration and the documented process.
