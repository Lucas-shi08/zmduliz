How your product
is actually made.

Production is planned around the approved product configuration, the process requirements and the inspection checkpoints. Testing scope depends on the approved product and the project requirement.

What we will not publish Equipment inventories, capacity figures and defect rates we cannot document. Ask for the reports that apply to the model you are considering and we will send those.

01 — Production

Four stages,
each with a checkpoint.

The production plan is built around the approved configuration. A stage closes before the next one opens — that is what makes the run repeatable.

01

Material & component control

Inputs are checked against the confirmed project requirements before assembly.

02

Process management

Production checkpoints support repeatability and issue traceability.

03

Assembly

Products follow the approved configuration and the documented process.

04

Final release

Inspection and packaging review precede outbound shipment.

02 — Quality control

Checked at the
stages that matter.

Four inspection points, from incoming material to final release. The scope of testing is set per product and per project — not promised as a fixed standard.

Quality inspection stages
01 Incoming inspectionCheck applicable components and materials before assembly
02 In-process controlDefined checkpoints during production
03 Acoustic verificationReview performance against the approved project target
04 Final inspectionConfirm product, appearance and packaging before release

Testing scope depends on the approved product and project requirement. Request the applicable reports for the model under discussion.

Documentation

Traceability
