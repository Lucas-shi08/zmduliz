02 — How we publish data

No unverified parameter is treated as an approved specification.

Most product pages in this industry are written to look complete. Ours are written to stay correct: a value appears only when the model file backs it, and the dash means we have not confirmed it yet.

That is a slower way to build a catalogue, and a faster way to avoid a claim you cannot defend when a customer measures the sample. Final specifications, fitment and packaging remain subject to model review — the approved sample governs the order.
