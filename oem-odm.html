01 — The process

Your ideas.
Our expertise.
Real products.

Seven steps from market requirement to mass production. Each one closes before the next opens — so the sample you approve is the product that ships.

  1. 01Define

    Market, buyer, price position and product concept — agreed before any tooling or sampling decision.

  2. 02Engineer

    Acoustic and structural design reviewed against the platform you selected.

  3. 03Tune

    Sound performance optimised within the platform and evaluated against the confirmed specification.

  4. 04Prototype

    Samples built and verified; the approved sample governs the order.

  5. 05Brand

    Logo application, finish and styling on the product, the accessories and the packaging.

  6. 06Package

    Packaging, accessory set and artwork designed for your market, subject to the approved sample.

  7. 07Produce

    Mass production with in-process quality control and final release inspection.

Production itself runs through material and component control, process management, assembly and final release.
